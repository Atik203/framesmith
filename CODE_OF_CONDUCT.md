# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Framesmith spaces should be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. We welcome
people regardless of background or experience. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation,
and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Examples of helpful conduct include giving specific, considerate feedback, accepting corrections gracefully,
and discussing disagreements about ideas without attacking people.

## Scope

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing the
Framesmith project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to the maintainer through the contact method on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Atik203). For safety concerns, avoid posting private details
publicly. The maintainer will review reports as time permits and may edit or remove contributions, close
discussions, or restrict participation. The maintainer will handle reports with discretion and will share
details only as needed to address them.
